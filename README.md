# KPI Nexus – Executive KPI Intelligence Dashboard

KPI Nexus is an AI-powered Executive KPI Intelligence platform designed to help organizations monitor business performance across multiple domains, identify unusual KPI patterns, understand cross-domain relationships, forecast future trends and support executive decision-making.

## 🚀 Live Demo

**Website:** https://kpi-nexus.vercel.app/

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Organizations often monitor KPIs across different business areas such as Finance, Workforce, Customer Experience and Projects. However, analyzing these KPIs separately can make it difficult for executives to identify important trends, risks and relationships.

KPI Nexus provides a centralized executive dashboard that brings these business insights together in one platform.

The dashboard presents:

- Key business performance indicators
- KPI trends
- Anomaly detection results
- Forecasting results
- Cross-domain intelligence
- AI-generated recommendations
- Executive reports

---

## 🎯 Objectives

KPI Nexus aims to:

- Centralize KPI monitoring across multiple business domains
- Identify abnormal KPI patterns early
- Forecast future KPI trends
- Reveal relationships between different business KPIs
- Provide actionable recommendations
- Support faster and more informed executive decision-making

## 🏢 Business Domains

KPI Nexus covers four major business domains:

### 💰 Financial

Monitors financial performance indicators such as:

- Revenue

### 👥 Workforce

Monitors workforce-related indicators such as:

- Attendance
- Productivity

### 😊 Customer Experience

Monitors customer-related indicators such as:

- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Response Time

### 📋 Projects

- Project Completion
- Budget
- Schedule Performance

## 🤖 AI & Analytics Capabilities

### Anomaly Detection

KPI Nexus uses anomaly detection to identify unusual patterns in business KPI data.

Detected anomalies can help executives identify potential business risks that may require further investigation.

### KPI Forecasting

Forecasting is used to estimate future KPI trends and support business planning.

The dashboard presents forecast results through interactive visualizations.

### Cross-Domain Intelligence

KPI Nexus analyzes relationships between KPIs across different business domains.

This helps identify potential relationships between business areas that may not be obvious when KPIs are analyzed independently.

### AI Recommendations

The platform presents actionable recommendations based on the detected KPI issues and analytical findings.

These recommendations help translate analytical results into potential business actions.
